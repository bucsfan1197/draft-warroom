Mothew Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Who uses the app

Mothew Studio is used only by the owner of the Mothew accounts to publish their own videos. It has no public sign-up and no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Authorization tokens issued by TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and YouTube (Google) when the owner connects their own account. These are used only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to read basic profile information and performance statistics of the owner's own posts.
  • Basic profile information of the owner's connected accounts (such as account ID, username and display name).

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data about any other people, including viewers, followers or commenters.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising.

YouTube API Services

Mothew Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to read basic statistics about those videos. By using Mothew Studio with YouTube, the account owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is handled in accordance with this policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. Stored tokens are deleted from the owner's computer when access is revoked or no longer needed, and no YouTube data is kept for longer than 30 days without being refreshed.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's private computer and are never uploaded to any server operated by Mothew Studio. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok, Instagram or Google account settings.
